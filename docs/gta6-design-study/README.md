# GTA 6 Design Study

A working reference on how Rockstar builds *places* and *people* in Grand Theft Auto VI
(Leonida / Vice City), assembled from public material: the official trailers and Extended
Look, Rockstar's own pre-release notes, and press breakdowns. It is written for people
designing a world of their own — a game, a film, a stylised app — not as a fan wiki.

## Caveat on sourcing

GTA VI is unreleased at the time of writing (announced for 19 November 2026). Everything
here is drawn from marketing material and press reporting, both of which show a curated
slice of the game. Treat specifics as *directional* — the design thinking is the durable
part, the individual details may not survive to release.

## The one-paragraph version

Vice City is not "Miami with the serial numbers filed off"; it is Miami studied by a
resident research team for years and then rebuilt as an argument about vanity, heat, and
money. The art direction runs two opposed palettes — sun-bleached daylight and neon night —
so the same street reads as two different emotional registers depending on the hour. The
state around the city exists to make the city mean something: swamp, sugar country,
mountain, industrial port and keys each strip away one thing the city has. And the people
are the load-bearing detail: appearance is treated as *evidence* — of neighbourhood, of
income, of taste, of what the character did last night.
